# MyCoWork 3D 浅蓝配色设计稿

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成。保留原版 3D 小牛形象，以当前 UI 的浅蓝、湖蓝、蓝灰为主色，柔粉色作为小面积点缀。已接入应用：主图位于 `renderer/src/assets/brand/app-logo.png`，启动页、标题栏、折叠侧栏及网页图标共用该资产；`python3 build/generate-icon.py` 导出 macOS PNG、Windows ICO 与 README 图标。新建聊天页使用以该角色为参考衍生的单智能体、多智能体两张场景插图，见 `README.md`。

## 最终提示词

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand. Edit target: the supplied MyCoWork 3D cow app icon. Create one refined, production-ready recolored version compatible with a calm blue-and-white desktop AI productivity app. PRESERVE the same lovable 3D cow identity, frontal head position, round head silhouette, broad floppy ears, two rounded horns, tuft of hair, asymmetrical left-eye patch, big expressive eyes, gentle smiling muzzle, original proportions, and rounded-square tile composition. Keep the polished softly sculpted 3D clay/vinyl look, realistic ambient occlusion, soft contact shadow, material volume and tasteful studio highlights. The character must remain cute and dimensional, never flat, vector, geometric, robotic or metallic. PALETTE: replace the original purple/pink/yellow background with a quiet icy-blue to lake-blue gradient, using #E1F3FF, #B6E2FC, #77BDDB and #007FCA, blending smoothly from light upper left to slightly richer blue lower right. The rounded tile should be opaque and have gently beveled edges, without any dark outline or thick frame. Cow face and outer ears: pearl white / neutral ivory, with very subtle cool-blue reflections from the tile. Recolor the brown eye patch, eyebrows, and hair tuft to rich blue-slate (#233E50, subtly lighter on highlights), retaining rounded 3D modeling. Eyes remain dark expressive eyes with clean bright highlights. Tone down the strong yellow horns to soft pale champagne ivory. Keep the muzzle and inner ears a gentle desaturated blush pink as a small restrained warm accent; no hot pink, bright orange or gold. Aim for a cohesive pale-blue and slate-blue brand palette with a warm approachable smile. Match the original image's clean close-up centered framing and generous cow head size; all ears and horns remain within the tile. Deliver a single square high-resolution icon, no text, no watermark, no extra symbols, no presentation board, no mockup, no other icons. Preserve genuine transparency outside the rounded-square silhouette; inside the tile the blue background is fully opaque.
