# Contract Drafting Playbook

Use this playbook for drafting contracts, clauses, user agreements, service terms, authorization documents, NDAs, cooperation agreements, paid-community terms, AI service agreements, and developer licenses.

## Intake Questions

Ask only missing material questions:

1. Who signs with whom?
2. What is the transaction object/service/deliverable?
3. Amount, payment node, invoice requirement?
4. Delivery and acceptance criteria?
5. Term and renewal/termination?
6. IP ownership and permitted use?
7. Confidential information and data processing?
8. Liability cap, indemnity, liquidated damages?
9. Dispute forum and governing law?
10. Which side should the draft favor?

## Required Clauses

For most commercial contracts include:

- parties;
- definitions if needed;
- scope/deliverables;
- price/payment/invoice/tax;
- delivery/acceptance;
- cooperation obligations;
- IP and materials;
- confidentiality;
- data/privacy/security;
- representations and warranties;
- breach/liability cap/indemnity;
- term/termination/effects of termination;
- force majeure/change control;
- notice/electronic delivery;
- governing law/dispute resolution;
- attachments/SOW/order priority.

## Drafting Rules

- Prefer clear operational duties over abstract legal language.
- Put measurable acceptance standards into the contract when possible.
- Tie payment to objective deliverables and evidence.
- For AI/content services, define responsibility for generated output review, AI labels, copyright, prompt/material ownership, third-party rights, and user/customer data.
- For medical beauty, finance, insurance, education, or other regulated customers, add regulatory compliance and customer-side approval obligations.
- For developer/Skill licensing, define license scope, attribution, redistribution, derivative works, revenue share, termination, and audit rights.
