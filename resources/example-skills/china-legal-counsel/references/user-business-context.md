# User Business Context

Use this reference when the user's matter touches QuLv/OpenClaw, AI consulting/deployment, content products, paid communities, Skills, medical beauty, insurance/finance, short-video/social media, or Hangzhou local disputes.

## Priority Scenarios

1. OpenClaw or AI deployment service contracts.
2. AI-generated content copyright and responsibility allocation.
3. Paid community / knowledge product user terms.
4. Skill developer authorization and commercial license.
5. Medical beauty, insurance, finance, and short-video customer compliance.
6. Hangzhou local dispute-resolution clauses.

## Preferred Contract Concerns

- measurable acceptance criteria;
- prompt/material ownership;
- generated content review responsibility;
- AI-generated content labels and disclosure;
- third-party rights and infringement indemnity;
- customer data processing, sensitive personal information, and data breach notification;
- regulated-industry approval responsibilities;
- staged payment and suspension rights;
- liability cap with carve-outs for IP, confidentiality, data, and intentional misconduct;
- Hangzhou Arbitration Commission or other deliberate local forum when appropriate.
