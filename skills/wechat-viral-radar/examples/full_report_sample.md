- Confirmed `10万+` evidence should be separated from suspected viral candidates.
- Best immediate writing direction: combine a current topic with a concrete ordinary-person scenario.

## Creative Angle Bank

- Ordinary-person experiment: "I tried this for 7 days..."
- Mistake teardown: "Most people use X wrong..."
- Decision checklist: "Before you choose X, check these 5 things..."
- Counterintuitive answer: "The real opportunity is not X, but Y..."

## 3-Day Publishing Plan

| Day | Content Type | Title Direction | Goal |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hot topic explainer | What changed today? | Catch search and share demand. |
| 2 | Practical guide | How ordinary people can use it | Convert attention into saves. |
| 3 | Opinion teardown | What most people misunderstand | Build account point of view. |

## Risk Notes

- Do not copy titles directly.
- Finance, medical, legal, and policy topics need cautious wording.
- Treat suspected viral results as references, not precise ranking data.
